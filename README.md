## 📌 About the Project

Sharda Medical Store is a business-oriented website designed to strengthen the pharmacy's digital presence and make healthcare product information and pharmacy services easier to access.

The website provides an online product catalog, medicine enquiry options, WhatsApp-based ordering, pharmacy information, and dedicated pages for customers looking to learn about the store or contact its team.

The project focuses on creating a practical, user-friendly digital experience that connects traditional pharmacy services with modern web technology.

## ✨ Key Features

* 📱 **Responsive Design** — Optimized for browsing across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
* 💊 **Pharmacy Product Catalog** — Explore prescription medicines, healthcare and wellness products, and medical equipment.
* 🔎 **Product Discovery** — Browse product categories and find relevant items in the catalog.
* 💬 **WhatsApp Ordering** — Send medicine enquiries and order requests directly to the pharmacy through WhatsApp.
* 🧾 **Prescription Enquiries** — Contact the pharmacy regarding prescription requirements and medicine availability.
* 🚚 **Home Delivery Information** — Learn about medicine delivery services and how to place an order.
* 🏥 **Wholesale Enquiries** — Dedicated information for doctors, clinics, and healthcare providers.
* 📍 **Business Information** — Access the store's location, operating hours, contact details, and services.
* ❓ **Frequently Asked Questions** — Find answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and pharmacy services.
* 📄 **Dedicated Website Pages** — Separate pages for Home, Shop, About, Contact, FAQs, and relevant policy information.
* 🔍 **SEO-Friendly Content** — Location-focused page content and metadata to support search visibility.
* 🚀 **Live Deployment** — Hosted on Vercel for public access to the website.
